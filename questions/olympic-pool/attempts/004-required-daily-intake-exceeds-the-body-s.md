1. 🏊 (root) — Given: an Olympic pool holds a few million litres.
2. 🧓 (root) — Given: a long lifetime is around eighty years, which is tens of thousands of days.
3. 🥤 (root) — Given: a person normally drinks roughly a couple of litres of water a day.
4. 🫙 (root) — Given: a human stomach comfortably holds about a litre of liquid, and a few litres is the most it can be stretched to.
5. 🔁 (← 1, 2, 4) — Sharing the pool across all those days means emptying about a hundred stomach-fills every day, which makes the stomach the unit of the plan.
6. 🕰️ (← 5) — A hundred stomach-fills a day means a full refill roughly every quarter of an hour, with no break for sleep, because the day has only a few dozen quarter-hours.
7. 🛌 (← 6) — This pace would have to continue through the night and through illness, every day for eighty years, with no holiday.
8. 🧪 (root) — Given: the gut can pass into the blood only about a litre of water an hour at best, because water leaves the stomach and is absorbed at a limited speed.
9. 💥 (← 6, 8) — A refill every quarter hour is about four litres an hour, several times what the gut can absorb. This contradicts the gut's limit, so the plan cannot be carried out.
10. 🤪 (← 4, 9) — Even an extreme drinker who stretches the stomach to a few litres cannot absorb the water any faster, so the contradiction remains.
11. 🧍 (← 3, 5) — A normal person takes only about two stomach-fills a day, so the plan needs dozens of times the normal habit, and the pool is larger than a lifetime of normal drinking by that same factor.
12. 👀 (← 7, 10) — Self-check: the verdict does not depend on exact stomach size, because even a stomach twice as big leaves a refill every half hour all night, which is still impossible.
13. 🚫 (← 9, 11, 12) — So no one could drink a pool's worth of water in one lifetime.
14. 🔢 (← 11, 13) — The pool exceeds a lifetime of normal drinking by a factor in the tens, about forty.

LANDMARKS:
- The pool equals roughly a hundred stomach-fills every day for a whole lifetime.
- That means a full stomach refill every quarter hour, day and night, which no one could keep up.
- The gut absorbs only about a litre of water an hour, several times less than that pace needs.

ANSWER: No; the pool is bigger by roughly 40 times.
