# Question

Could one person, over an entire lifetime, drink as much water as an Olympic swimming pool holds?
Answer yes or no, and say roughly how many times larger the bigger quantity is than the smaller.

# Reference answer (referee only)

No. An Olympic pool holds about 2.5 million litres; a person drinks on the order of 2–3 litres a day,
which over 70–90 years is roughly 50,000–100,000 litres. The pool is larger by roughly 25–50 times.

**Tolerance:** any stated factor from about 10 to about 100 is correct. A chain that counts all
household water use rather than drinking answers a different question.
