# Question

From a standard 8×8 chessboard, the two corners at the same end of one row — a1 and h1 — are removed.
**Can the remaining 62 squares be exactly covered by 31 dominoes?**

# Reference answer (referee only)

**Yes.** a1 and h1 are opposite colours (a row has even length), so the colour count balances; and by
Gomory's argument a Hamiltonian cycle through the board, cut at two opposite-coloured squares, leaves
two even paths, each tileable end to end. Tolerance: yes/no exact; a route must give a reason that a
tiling exists, not merely that the colour objection fails.
