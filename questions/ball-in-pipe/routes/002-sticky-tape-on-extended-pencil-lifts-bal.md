CHAIN by blind player 2
1. 🏓 (root) — The ball is light, hollow, smooth and slightly compressible.
2. 🧪 (root) — The pipe is narrow, rigid, open at the top and closed by the floor.
3. 🏢 (root) — An office has tape, pencils, string and similar items.
4. ❓ (← 1, 2, 3) — How can the ball be removed without damaging the pipe or the ball, and what principle does the method use? This route must differ from the "fill with water" route.
5. 🧲 (← 4) — I choose adhesion as the principle: something sticky on the end of a stick can pull the ball up.
6. 🩹 (← 3, 5) — Wrap office tape around a pencil tip with the sticky side facing out. It makes a sticky tip and peels off cleanly.
7. ✏️ (← 2, 3) — A pencil is thinner than the pipe's bore, so it fits down the pipe. If the pencil is too short, tape a second pencil or a ruler to it to reach the bottom.
8. ⚖️ (← 1) — The ball weighs very little, so only a weak grip is needed to hold it.
9. 👇 (← 6, 7) — Lower the sticky tip until it touches the top of the ball, and press lightly. The ball is slightly compressible and the touch is gentle, so it is not harmed.
10. 🔗 (← 5, 8, 9) — The tape sticks to the smooth ball surface, and that adhesive force is larger than the ball's small weight.
11. ⬆️ (← 10) — Lift the stick slowly and the ball rises up the pipe with it.
12. 🤲 (← 11) — At the top, take the ball by hand and peel the tape off gently.
13. 👀 (← 12) — Self-check: the pipe was never touched or altered, and the ball only met light tape pressure, so neither is damaged.

LANDMARKS:
- A pencil is thinner than the pipe's bore, so it can reach the ball through the gap, and taping pencils together extends its length.
- Tape wrapped sticky side out on the stick makes a tip that bonds to the smooth ball without damaging it.
- The ball's weight is smaller than the adhesive force, so the ball hangs from the tip when it is lifted.
- The ball is lifted straight out by the stick, with no water or air flow involved.

ANSWER: Wrap tape sticky side out on a pencil tip (extend it by taping on a second pencil or a ruler if needed), lower it onto the ball, press lightly so it sticks, and lift the ball out. The principle is adhesion: the sticky force on the ball is greater than its tiny weight.
