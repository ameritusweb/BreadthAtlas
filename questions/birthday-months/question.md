# Question

Twenty-three people are in a room. **What is the probability that at least two of them were born in
the same calendar month?**

# Reference answer (referee only)

**Exactly 1.** There are twelve months and twenty-three people, so by the pigeonhole principle some
month holds at least two. No assumption about the distribution of birthdays is needed. Tolerance:
exact (1, 100%, or "certain"). The famous 50.7% is WRONG; any answer below 1 is WRONG.
