# Question

A wire-frame cube (edges only) is viewed in orthographic projection exactly along a space diagonal —
from one corner, through the centre, to the opposite corner. **What shape is the outline of the figure,
and how many of the twelve edges lie on that outline?**

# Reference answer (referee only)

**A regular hexagon; 6 edges.** The near and far corners coincide at the centre; their six edges
appear as six evenly spaced spokes; the remaining six edges join the six outer corners in a ring,
which is the outline. Tolerance: shape exact (regular hexagon); count exact.
