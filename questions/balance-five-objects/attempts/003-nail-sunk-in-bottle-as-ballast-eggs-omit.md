CHAIN by blind player 1
1. 🥚💻📖 (root) — Givens: nine eggs, a closed-flat laptop and a book are available, and the laptop and book are rigid and flat.
2. 🍷 (root) — Given: an open wine bottle, part full, is a tall cylinder that is top-heavy when upright.
3. 📌 (root) — Given: a nail is a thin steel spike, and it is small enough to pass through a bottle's neck.
4. ❓ (← 1, 2, 3) — Asked: arrange only these objects so the whole arrangement resists toppling, and state the principle of stability it relies on.
5. 🫙 (← 3, 2) — The nail is dropped through the open neck into the bottle. Enclosing it removes one awkward object that cannot be balanced on its own.
6. 🪨 (← 5) — Steel is denser than wine, so the nail sinks to the bottom of the liquid and stays there when the bottle tilts.
7. ⬇️ (← 6, 2) — The nail adds a little weight at the very bottom, and the wine is already low, so the bottle's centre of mass sits lower than the empty bottle's.
8. 💻 (← 1) — The laptop is closed and laid flat on the table as a wide, rigid platform.
9. 🔺 (← 8) — The bottle's flat base rests on the platform. A broad base gives many support points, so the support area is much wider than the bottle's neck.
10. 📖 (← 1, 9) — The book lies flat beside the bottle on the laptop, so it does not add height. It also shares the load across the platform and can catch a slight lean as a stop.
11. ⚓ (← 7, 9, 10) — Everything is low or lying down, and the heaviest part, the liquid and nail, is at the bottom. A tip must first raise the centre of mass, so small nudges fall back.
12. 👀 (← 11) — Self-check: the eggs are left out because they roll and are fragile to point loads. The question does not require using every object, so leaving them out lowers the risk of toppling.

LANDMARKS:
- The nail is enclosed in the bottle (containment), so one unbalanceable object is neutralized.
- The nail, denser than the wine, sinks to the bottom and adds low ballast.
- Eggs are deliberately left out, because they roll and are fragile to point loads.

ANSWER: Drop the nail into the bottle, where it sinks and adds low weight under the wine. Stand the bottle upright on a closed laptop with the book lying flat beside it. The arrangement resists toppling by containment of the nail plus a low centre of mass over a wide base.
