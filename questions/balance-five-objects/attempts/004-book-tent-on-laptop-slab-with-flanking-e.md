CHAIN by blind player 2
1. 🥚 (root) — Nine eggs: ovoid, strong under compression, roll on a flat surface.
2. 💻 (root) — A laptop: rigid and flat when closed.
3. 📖 (root) — A book: a rigid rectangle that can be opened into a V.
4. 🍷 (root) — An open wine bottle, part full: tall and cylindrical.
5. 📌 (root) — A nail: a thin rigid spike.
6. ❓ (← 1, 2, 3, 4, 5) — Using only these five kinds of object, arrange all of them so the whole arrangement resists toppling, and name the stability principle it relies on.
7. 🔺 (← 3) — An open book stood spine-up forms an inverted V. Its two covers and the surface beneath make a triangle, which cannot change shape without something bending, so it resists being pushed sideways. This is a new spine for the answer.
8. 💻 (← 2) — The laptop is closed and laid flat on the table as a rigid slab, giving a firm, level surface that the book does not scratch or slide on easily.
9. 📖 (← 7, 8) — The book tent stands on the slab, resting on the two cover edges, and the spine is its highest point. A push from either side presses one cover harder into the slab and cannot fold the triangle.
10. 🥚 (← 1, 9) — Eggs rest in the two long wedge-shaped corners where a cover meets the slab. The sloped cover stops an egg from rolling sideways away from the tent. The eggs press along their sides, not on points.
11. 🤝 (← 10) — Eggs in a row touch one another and the cover, so each is held by its neighbours and the cover. A little rolling along the corner moves only the eggs, and the eggs are low, so it does not topple the arrangement.
12. 🍷 (← 4, 8) — The bottle stands on the slab beside the tent, outside the egg corners. It is the tallest object, but it stays on the wide slab rather than on the eggs or the tent, and is not part of the load.
13. 📌 (← 5, 8) — The nail lies on its side on the slab. A lying nail has nowhere lower to fall, so it adds no toppling risk.
14. 🔺 (← 9, 11, 12, 13) — The tent is the only structure that has to carry or resist sideways load. Everything else rests on or beside it at slab level, so the support relies on this one triangle.
15. 👀 (← 14) — Self-check: if the bottle were nudged, it would tip against the slab, not the tent. The tent and eggs stay as they were, and the arrangement does not collapse.

LANDMARKS:
- An open book stood spine-up forms a triangle with the surface and resists sideways pushes without any other support.
- The laptop is used only as a closed flat slab and the book as a tent on it, so the book's triangulation is the structural spine.
- Eggs sit in the wedge between a sloping cover and the slab, which stops sideways rolling, and they touch one another.
- The bottle stands apart on the slab, so nothing relies on it for stability.

ANSWER: Close the laptop flat and stand the open book spine-up as a tent on it (triangulation). Lay the eggs in the two wedge corners beside the covers, stand the bottle on the slab beside the tent, and lay the nail flat on the slab. The arrangement resists toppling because the tent's triangle cannot fold and every other object sits low or lies flat.
