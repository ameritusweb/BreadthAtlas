# Question

Four labelled vertices form a non-flat tetrahedron. You are given every pairwise distance, but no positions or orientation-sensitive measurements. Relative to a fixed external orientation, can those distances uniquely determine the tetrahedron's handedness?

# Reference answer (referee only)

No. Reflect any compatible configuration. All corresponding pairwise distances stay unchanged while the labelled tetrahedron's handedness reverses. Thus the data permit opposite answers. Tolerance: exact no, supported by an observation-preserving transformation. Labels and the fixed external orientation are essential to defining the ask.
